About Me

Data for evidence-based decisions.

I'm a Health Information Systems and Data Specialist with a doctoral background in Information Technology and over eight years strengthening routine data systems, monitoring and evaluation, and the use of data for evidence-based decision-making across health and development programmes.

I'm currently embedded within the World Health Organization (WHO AFRO, Dakar Hub) as a Machine Learning and Disease Modelling Specialist, supporting 47 countries through data analysis, visualization, and triangulation — and building country capacity through dashboards, technical guides, training manuals, and Training-of-Trainers approaches.

My expertise spans cloud engineering, advanced analytics, and responsible AI. I've led the development of data lakehouses and pipelines, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) document retrieval systems, NLP pipelines for online content extraction, and mission-critical outbreak intelligence platforms — working with governments, regional bodies, and UN partners.
