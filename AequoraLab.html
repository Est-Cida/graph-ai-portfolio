01 — About the page

Built for a final project, grounded in real supply data.

This one-page site was produced for the Generative AI for Work & Research course. It turns a raw outbound shipment spreadsheet into a clear, browsable product catalogue.

Aequora Labs is the fictional storefront wrapped around a genuine dataset of laboratory and emergency-health products moving through a humanitarian warehouse. Every figure on this page — counts, prices, temperature requirements — is read directly from that file.

The goal was to practise the full loop: take messy operational data, clean and structure it, then present it in something a person would actually want to use. No frameworks, no templates — just hand-written HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript.

Browse below by category, search by name, and inspect the cold-chain and shipping-mode tags carried on each item.
